# How we treat each other here

SpriteScout comes from Scratch, so it keeps Scratch's habits. In issues, pull
requests and anywhere else in this project:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- **Be constructive.** "This breaks when the title has emoji" beats "this is
  broken".
- **Be honest.** Don't claim someone else's work as yours, and don't make up
  results.
- **Keep it friendly for everyone.** People here are all ages. No swearing, no
  insults, nothing you wouldn't post on Scratch itself.
- **Leave people's private things alone.** Don't post anyone's real name,
  address, school, or anything else they haven't shared themselves.

Anything that would get you in trouble under the
[Scratch Community Guidelines](https://scratch.mit.edu/community_guidelines)
isn't welcome here either.

If someone breaks these, tell me by opening an issue. I'll hide or delete the
comment, and block anyone who keeps at it.
